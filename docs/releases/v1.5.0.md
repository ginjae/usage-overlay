## Added

- **Check for Updates…** is available directly in the details menu, aligned with **Settings…**.

## Changed

- **Liquid Glass** is now the default overlay background on supported Macs. Your first launch after updating switches the background once; any background you choose afterward is kept. Other overlay settings are preserved.
- Overlay backgrounds are simplified to **Liquid Glass**, **Glass**, and **Solid**. Liquid Glass requires macOS 26 or later; Glass and Solid remain available on older Macs.
- Liquid Glass has a clearer surface, reflective edges, and pointer feedback.

## Fixed

- Liquid Glass keeps its active appearance when you focus another app. This uses an AppKit private appearance selector; future macOS changes may require a compatibility update.
- Rounded overlay corners no longer leave stray material outside the panel's outline.
- Codex reset links provide hover feedback.

**Full changelog:** https://github.com/ginjae/usage-overlay/compare/v1.4.0...v1.5.0
