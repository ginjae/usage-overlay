## Added

- **Codex reset status** in the details menu shows the latest reset, scheduled announcements, and AI forecasts from [Codex Resets](https://codex-resets.com). Compact rows link to the original posts, with their text available on hover.

## Changed

- Experimental Codex reset status is on by default. You can turn it off in **Settings → Details → Experimental**.

- The details menu resizes when refreshed content grows or shrinks, removing leftover blank space.
